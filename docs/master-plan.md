# SwarmLLM Master Plan

## 1. The Thesis

SwarmLLM is the first compute network where the contributor is the beneficiary in the same session: open a link, and our tabs together run a model none of us can run alone. It cannot win on price (Qwen3-32B is already $0.08/$0.28 per Mtok — openrouter.ai/qwen/qwen3-32b) or speed (Groq ~478 tok/s, Cerebras ~1,700+ — infrabase.ai), so it doesn't compete there. The product is **no counterparty**: no account, no API key, no retention policy to trust — privacy by architecture, honestly scoped to "private among your friends." Why now: WebGPU is default-on (~82% of browsers), 25 years of browser volunteer computing failed only because the volunteer got no direct utility (arxiv.org/pdf/1910.07402), Petals proved stranger-swarms die on WAN bandwidth and trust while never making the friends-room pivot (explainx.ai Petals revisited, July 2026), and exo left the zero-install cross-network lane empty by choosing installed, LAN-first, Apple-first. Category name: **"Ollama for groups"** — a legible category with proven adoption, not "a Petals revival," a category with proven failure.

## 2. NOW (this week — the open-source launch)

Ordered. Items 1–3 gate the launch; do not go public without 1.

1. **TURN fallback.** The viral failure mode is "I joined and nothing happened" — F@h's work-unit shortage lesson: spikes convert only as fast as onboarding allows. Symmetric-NAT pairs silently fail today. Cheapest path: coturn on a $5 VPS now; later, the signaling broker doubles as a WebTransport relay (WebTransport is client-server only, so it can't replace the mesh — but it's the ideal relay).
2. **SECURITY.md / threat-model page** from the honest-claims cheat-sheet. Must say: "assume anyone in your room can read your prompts" — inversion attacks recover ~88% of tokens from mid-model activations, no safe split depth (arxiv.org/html/2503.09022v1). CAN say: peers never see your text, logits, or sampled tokens directly; trust model = sharing a doc link. CANNOT say: "private," "encrypted," "verified." This preempts the inevitable HN takedown and buys the privacy audience — candor is the moat against policy-not-architecture vendors (Groq ZDR is an opt-in console toggle; DeepInfra retains data post-inference).
3. **Coordinated one-shot launch.** Show HN Tue–Thu ~8–10am ET ("Run a 27B LLM across your friends' browser tabs"), r/LocalLLaMA, and X/LinkedIn to the 220k-impression audience the same day (HN exposure averages 121 stars/24h; timing beats the Show HN tag itself — arxiv.org/abs/2511.04453). README as product page: 15-second GIF of two laptops running 27B above the fold, the join one-liner, prior art named (Petals, exo, WebLLM) with the delta stated: zero-install, cross-network, models bigger than one device. The live demo link is the conversion path.
4. **Always-on public demo room + live status page.** Petals' dead health dashboard (health.petals.dev still times out, checked 2026-09-01) was its loudest death signal. One click, model already running — the spike must have something to do immediately.
5. **Landing page degrades to instant solo demo** (SmolLM/Qwen 0.6B, first token in seconds), then the hook: "this tab runs 0.6B alone; with 2 friends you'd run 27B" + one-click room link. Surface the Cache API explicitly on rejoin ("ready in 2s — your layers are cached").
6. **good-first-issue seeds**: WGSL kernels, model configs, UI. The from-scratch engine is the contributor magnet — publicize it independently of the network.

## 3. NEXT (weeks 2–6), ranked by impact/effort

1. **`npx swarmllm serve`** — highest-leverage feature, period. ~5MB bridge exposing `http://localhost:11435/v1/chat/completions` (SSE streaming, JSON mode) over WebRTC to the host tab, bound to 127.0.0.1 with an auto-generated bearer token printed as `OPENAI_API_KEY`, per-request attribution in the room UI ("nehanth's Continue.dev asked a question"). OpenAI-compatible is the universal socket — Ollama (11434) and LM Studio (1234) prove one base_url swap converts every tool (docs.ollama.com/openai). Architecture validated by web-p2p-tunnel (HN 40227403). A browser tab can't accept inbound TCP; this bridge is mandatory, not optional.
2. **Day-one integration docs** shipped with the bridge: Continue.dev in 4 lines of YAML (chat/edit roles only — honest about autocomplete latency; sub-second tab-completion needs local 1.5B models per Continue's own docs), "Open WebUI in 60 seconds," LiteLLM config snippet. The demo: "Continue.dev chat backed by 27B across three MacBooks."
3. **Swarm HUD + persistent browser-local stats.** Per-tab live counters (tokens served, layers held, uptime) — BOINC's proven retention engine ("participants are highly motivated by credit," Anderson 2004), zero backend. Persist lifetime stats in localStorage (tokens served, largest model helped run, peak tok/s). Add the Wordle-mechanic share card at the brag moment: plain text + site link, one click to copy ("SwarmLLM — Qwen3 27B across 3 devices, 9.2 tok/s, my MacBook served 4,812 tokens").
4. **BENCHMARKS.md + reproducible scripts** in industry vocabulary: tok/s decode/prefill per device class, TTFT including P2P round-trip, scaling curve vs. peer count, "$0.00/Mtok." Comparison sites rank on dated, verified numbers (infrabase.ai's 94-provider table); a row at $0 is free marketing no provider can match. Publish "9 tok/s on 27B beats Petals' best-ever 70B number by 50%."
5. **Mid-stream peer recovery.** A closing tab currently kills the session — consumer nodes churn; design for disconnection or die by it (Salad's entire model embraces churn). V1: hot-spare layer ranges assigned to peers with headroom (weights already cached on joiners).
6. **Room queueing + browser-local kudos.** AI Horde's template exactly: earned hosting layers plus an availability drip, spent only on queue priority, giftable, never sellable (haidra-assets kudos.md). Breaks the one-question bottleneck with no accounts and no money.
7. **Qwen3.5 ladder (2B/9B) + Qwen3.6-27B text mode** — config-only on existing GDN kernels (3.5/3.6/3.8 share the identical hybrid architecture; gains were post-training — codersera Qwen3.5 guide). 9B fills the 4B–27B gap; 2B gives weak devices a role; 3.6-27B is rated the best all-round local model. Also list "DeepSeek V4 (distilled)" free once 9B lands (Jackrong/Qwen3.5-9B-DeepSeek-V4-Flash-GGUF) — the top-of-OpenRouter brand without touching MLA.
8. **Persistent room links** (`swarmllm.ai/r/qwen-27b-fridays`) remembering model + layer assignments so rejoin re-attaches instantly from cache. Jackbox's room-code mechanic works identically for friend groups and streamers — persistence turns "a session you lose" into "our swarm."
9. **`@swarmllm/client`** npm package, WebLLM-API-shaped (WebLLM set the expected surface: `chat.completions.create` with OpenAI parameter names, 18.7k stars) — drop-in for WebLLM users and pure-web apps.
10. **Canary spot-checks** — a weekend of work: precomputed reference activations per model, host occasionally interleaves a randomized canary, relative-L2 tolerance (never exact-match — heterogeneous GPUs and per-device autotune make honest peers differ bit-for-bit). AUROC 1.000 in exactly this P2P layer-split setting (arxiv.org/html/2607.19490v1). Claim "randomly audited," never "verified." Petals proposed this and never shipped it — SwarmLLM would be the only P2P inference system with integrity checking actually live.
11. **Land MTP self-speculation** (1.3–1.7x) if it lands free; it's a multiplier, not a milestone.

Also now, cheap: an application-level framing layer for DataChannels (16KiB chunks, parallel channels) — Chromium hard-closes channels on messages >256KiB (lgrahl.de); KV handoff and MoE routing will hit this. Build it before production discovers it.

## 4. LATER (months 2–3) — the ambitious bets

- **Headline: Qwen3.5-122B-A10B expert-split across a room.** Implement MoE routing once: 35B-A3B (~3B active) as the speed demo, 122B-A10B as "a 122B model no consumer device can hold, in browser tabs" — the screenshot that goes viral twice. Fine-grained 512-dim experts fit expert-parallel splitting; host already runs sampling so it naturally runs the router; GGUF experts are contiguous tensors so range-fetch already works. Layer-split and expert-split compose. The 2025–26 edge-MoE literature (WDMoE, OD-MoE, arxiv 2508.12851) maps directly onto room-of-friends topology.
- **PipeInfer-style continuous speculation** — fill pipeline bubbles WITH speculative runs, early-cancel by dropping queued WebRTC messages (arxiv.org/abs/2407.11798, up to 2.15x, explicitly robust to low-bandwidth interconnects). A scheduling change, not new kernels — could push 27B decode well past 9 tok/s.
- **EdgeShard-style DP placement** (arxiv 2405.14371): autotune already measures per-device throughput; add pairwise RTT/bandwidth probes at join and optimize placement — including excluding a phone-class peer from the critical path. Galaxy-style hybrid parallelism for LAN-local pairs (tensor-split two machines on one WiFi into one virtual fast stage, pipeline across WAN).
- **CacheGen-style compressed KV migration** (3.5–4.3x, SIGCOMM 2024): live handoff of a departing peer's KV + layers. Converts "fragile demo" into "resilient mesh" — worth more than raw tok/s.
- **Subgroup kernels**: shipped stable in Chrome 134, 2.3–2.9x on GEMV (developer.chrome.com/blog/new-in-webgpu-134); autotuner selects subgroup path with shared-memory fallback. Safari subgroups landed in STP 249 — test now so M-class Macs (the 6.7 tok/s cohort) upgrade transparently day one. Prototype subgroup-matrix prefill behind a flag (tensor-core-class hardware coming to WGSL).
- **TOPLOC-style fingerprint spot-checks** (258 bytes per 32 tokens, catches model/precision substitution — arxiv 2501.16007) as verification step two after canaries.
- **Classroom/lab anchor + hackathon launch event.** Campus GPU utilization averages <35% (ReclaimNet); "turn a computer lab into one 27B for a class period" is the structural best-fit v2 story — trusted LAN, identical machines, zero IT. Sponsor one university hackathon compute room: if it survives a hackathon LAN, it survives a computer lab.
- **Long-context marketing**: 75% GDN linear layers make 256K context memory-cheap in exactly the way browser tabs need — "drop a codebase into a 27B across three laptops," plus a thinking-budget control for Qwen3.8's documented overthinking (simonwillison.net Aug 2026).
- Stretch, only if bandwidth allows: `window.ai`/Prompt-API-shaped shim (apps written for Chrome's Gemini Nano transparently upgrade to swarm-backed bigger models, cross-browser).

## 5. NEVER

- **Accounts.** The no-counterparty property is the product; localStorage identity covers stats, kudos, and streaks.
- **Money, tokens, crypto.** Paying for unverified compute is a documented fraud magnet — io.net's ~1.8M phantom GPUs, ~2% of advertised supply actually usable (theblock.co/post/291315). Kudos stay priority-only and unsellable, in the ToS from day one.
- **Open global stranger swarm.** Petals' post-mortem: WAN activation-passing starves on residential bandwidth, and "Can't wait to run a Sybil farm... and log all the juicy prompts" (HN 49015735). SwarmLLM is a LAN party, not a torrent. Public tier only ever as a clearly-labeled experiment gated behind canary auditing.
- **Training.** The flagship recentralized (INTELLECT-3 trained on 512 H200s), the verification company paused (Gensyn), the working system permissioned (Psyche). Inference-first among trusted peers is the correct read of the field.
- **Noise/permutation "privacy features."** Security theater: sigma=1 Gaussian noise still leaves 79.5% reconstruction while wrecking output; permutation schemes broken "near-perfectly" at ICML 2025 (arxiv 2505.18332). Shipping them would be dishonest and invite a takedown.
- **Native DeepSeek/GLM-5/Kimi-class frontier models.** ~170GB FP8-native GGUFs are out of browser-swarm range; distills capture the brand for free.
- **Llama 4, vision encoder, mobile native apps, SLAs, global public leaderboards with accounts.** Llama 3.2 1B/3B for onboarding familiarity is fine (119M Ollama pulls prove the mental model); Llama 4 is eclipsed and license-encumbered. Vision waits until the text mesh is resilient.
- **Claiming "verified," "trustless," or "private" beyond friends.** zkML is 10^3–10^4x too slow (~15 min to prove one 13B forward pass), browsers have no TEE surface. Probabilistic auditing is the honest ceiling.

## 6. The Moat

**Vs. providers:** They can't offer no-counterparty — accounts and billing ARE their business. Swarm-first routing with paid fallback is a feature no provider copies without cannibalizing revenue. Byte-level auditability (the exact GGUF SHA runs, displayed per session) answers the documented silent-quantization trust problem no API can. "Your model can never be deprecated" hits a real pain (Cerebras: 3-model catalog, preview models killed within a product cycle) and uniquely serves personal fine-tunes no provider will ever host. HF is a channel, not a competitor — it has no browser/P2P execution story at all; SwarmLLM is the client-side provider for the Hub.

**Vs. P2P projects:** (1) The from-scratch WGSL engine — one of the only engines running Gated DeltaNet hybrids in a browser at all; years of kernel work a fork can't shortcut. (2) Zero-install: the room link IS the onboarding — Petals, exo, and F@h all bled users at the installer, and no native tool does "your gaming-PC friend joins from a Discord link, cross-network, cross-OS." (3) The friends trust model sidesteps the verification/incentive swamp that consumed everyone who entered it. (4) Contributor-is-beneficiary in the same session — the configuration 25 years of browser volunteer computing never had. The community moat compounds: every room shared is onboarding someone else paid nothing for.
