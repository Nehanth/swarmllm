# SwarmLLM: exact decode across many devices — ranked report on 33 candidate ideas

> Generated 2026-09-04 by a 107-agent review: 7 generators (numerics, systems, speculation, architecture, distributed, coding theory, contrarian), 3 skeptics per idea (exactness, latency math, prior art), one synthesis. Skeptics ran real experiments where they could (parareal-over-depth on SmolLM-135M and Qwen3-0.6B on the GB10; dcSCTP source audit). Constraint for every idea: the token stream must be identical to the serial forward pass.


**Bottom line up front.** No idea on the table computes an exact (bit-identical or tolerance-certified) token for a single stream in less than (number of serial cuts) × (one-way hop) time. The skeptics converged on the same three facts from six independent directions, and they hold for every parallel-in-depth, wavefront, asynchronous, surrogate, and replication idea:

- **Wavefront lemma.** For x_d = F_d(x_{d−1}), any iterate at device d after k exchange rounds depends only on the initial guesses at boundaries d−k..d; token t's embedding reaches device D at round D, whatever the iteration (Jacobi, parareal, Newton, Anderson, secant, surrogate, async). Residual blocks pass a 1-ulp input change straight through the identity path (200/200 perturbations changed f16 output bits), so bit-exact fixed points appear at round D+1, never earlier. Measured on SmolLM-135M and Qwen3-0.6B (scripts in scratchpad `depth_jacobi2.py`): K = 0.8–1.0·D for Jacobi / identity-parareal / rank-8 secant / Anderson(6), min 0.7·D on any of 144 decode steps; per-block sensitivity β = 0.41–0.55 (7–16 at layer 0). SNLP (arXiv 2605.17842) reports σ_max ≈ 44 and divergent Jacobi on pretrained models; its Identity-Newton update is algebraically idea 1's variant (a).
- **Tolerance is not a certificate.** Pre-front iterates are the previous token's trajectory re-propagated: self-consistent (small residual), stable across rounds, and wrong (argmax = the token just consumed). A rigorous margin bound needs Lipschitz constants of up to 63 layers of softmax attention + DeltaNet gates (vacuous). Any DeltaNet S / KV entry committed from a tolerance-converged input poisons every later token with no residual signal. Seeded sampling flips whenever the draw lands in the shifted CDF interval, so divergence over a long generation is near-certain.
- **Replicas are not bit-identical in this engine.** `engine/autotune.js` picks the coop-GEMV shape per peer (different reduction tree even on identical phones); WGSL leaves exp/log/div/fma-contraction implementation-defined across Apple/Adreno/Mali/NVIDIA; `_dop()` selects coop(1)/coop_b4/coop_b8/split-K GEMM(16) by column count with different accumulation orders (repo docs: relDiff 6e-7..1.6e-6, "prefill was already not bit-identical to sequential"). First-arrival-wins samples a random rounding lineage per hop per token with no verify step; the f16 wire turns a 1-ulp fp32 disagreement into ~0.7–3.8 flipped wire elements per boundary.

What survives is exact by construction and attacks the constant, not the scaling: transport surgery, topology/placement, FEC, priming, lossless coding, tile cut-through. Combined they are plausibly 3–6× on a real clustered swarm with a bit-identical stream. The paper is the negative result plus the transport finding.

**Conventions.** g = 6 ms/layer (phone), o = 6.5–7 ms JS/hop, h = 100 ms/hop as measured. Serial lap D=64 (1 layer/phone): 64 × 112.5 = 7.2 s → 0.14 tok/s plain, ~0.6 tok/s with MTP (4.5 accepted/lap). D=4 (16 layers/phone): 4 × 202.5 = 0.81 s → 1.2 tok/s plain, ~4.5–5 tok/s with MTP (skeptics used 203 ms/token). Numbers below are skeptic-corrected, not the proposers'.

## 2. Cheapest experiments this week (DGX Spark + existing Deno engine)

**Experiment 0 (run first, closes the door on 21 of the 24 refuted ideas on the real model).** On the Spark, add a "stale-input block mode" to the Deno engine or dump all 64 boundary states for 300 greedy tokens over 5 prompts. Offline (NumPy, half a day): for each boundary j compute the relative guess error e_j = ‖x_j(t) − x_j(t−1)‖/‖x_j(t)‖ and the block sensitivity β_j = ‖F_j(x_prev) − F_j(x) − (x_prev − x)‖/‖x_prev − x‖; run chain Jacobi with previous-token seeding at D ∈ {4, 8, 16, 64} and record the round at which argmax stabilizes and the round at which every boundary is f16-bit-identical to the previous round. The scripts exist for HF models (`scratchpad/depth_jacobi2.py`, needs a transformers with Qwen3.5 support) and gave K = 0.8–1.0·D on SmolLM/Qwen3-0.6B. **Pass (family revived):** median β < 0.05 on ≥ 50 boundaries and argmax-stable round ≤ 8 at D=64. **Fail (expected, β ≈ 0.4–0.6):** the depth fixed-point / wavefront / async / surrogate families are closed for this model; the only residual is the "last ~7 devices in a star" hybrid (saves 15–20% of a lap).

## 3. What composes with what

**Transport stack (all exact, all multiply on the per-hop constant):** #20 pre-slicing/striping/warm cwnd + #7 per-symbol FEC on the unordered channel + #29 DPCM (fewer packets → fewer cwnd rounds) + #21 primes on a separate unordered channel + #22/#25's per-column or per-tile framing (one framing serves cut-through, FEC units and the ≤ 4.6 KB rule). Everything in the fixed-point family assumed a hop costs RTT/2; today's stack charges 1.5–5 RTT for 10–70 KB frames, so #20 is also the substrate any future lap-cutting scheme would need.

**Placement stack (exact):** #24/#8 cells with contiguous layers, host off-ring, token-id closing hop + #9 restricted to discrete-GPU desktop cell-heads absorbing 20–40 layers + #33's overlap coverage with the path fixed per generation (replicas as failover only, resynced from buffered boundary states, never racing) + the path-hedging variant of #23 (two ICE paths to the same device, first message wins). Rule for spare memory: spend it on fewer hops first, then on failover replicas, never on hedging (hedging is dominated by halving D in every skeptic's numbers). Conflict: #9 staging, #24 cell-heads and any replication compete for the same spare memory.

**With the fixed-point-over-depth idea already on the table:** negative. Every skeptic found K ≈ D, so composing it with cells only reduces the iterated boundary count from D to C and then reproduces the topology-sorted serial chain at C× GPU (#31). The single form that could survive Experiment 0 is the hybrid from idea 1's latency skeptic: exact serial chain for the first D−7 devices, star iteration over the last ~7 (parareal finished ~6–7 sweeps early at D = 28–30), worth ≤ 15–20% of a lap, not worth K× GPU. Any tolerance-converged state must be a draft only: never commit S/KV from it.

**With drafts-in-flight (idea 2 on the table):** this is the only route that decouples *throughput* from D × RTT, and it keeps latency at D × RTT. Useful engineering salvaged from the refuted ideas for it: the reorder-buffer/epoch bookkeeping and ReplaySSM ring snapshots (#11, #14), the version-vector consistent-cut certificate extended with a device state-version (#28), a separate unordered channel for prune/rollback messages (#28, #14), extending `S_shadow` from 7 to 15 slots (#5), and pipelining verify laps PipeInfer-style so bit-exact throughput is bounded by max per-device time (#5's fix). Prerequisite for all of them and for #10/#25: a batch-invariant kernel path (same per-column reduction order for N = 1..16), because `_dop()`'s width-dependent kernel selection breaks bit-identity whenever column count depends on timing.

**Dead-end compositions:** #27 with anything (re-sent exact columns cancel the byte savings); #6/#13/#23-stage/#26-striping/#33-hedging with anything (replica lemma) unless replica pairs pass a runtime bit-identity canary and autotune is pinned per room.

## 4. Blunt verdict

**Bet the paper on #20 (transport surgery) as the headline systems result, with Experiment 0's negative result as the theory section, and #24 as the second contribution.** Reasons: it is exact by construction; the diagnosis is verified against Chromium's dcSCTP source (rto_min 400 ms, initial cwnd 10 MTU, `max_burst` 4 with no drain timer, cwnd → 1 MTU after T3) and is stronger than the proposer claimed (a single 10 KB token frame from an idle association already pays ~1.5 RTT per hop; a K=5 verify block pays 3–5 RTT, which is a plausible explanation of the 100–200 ms cellular spread and of adaptive-K fighting the transport); the first fix is ~50 lines (pre-slice into ≤ 4.6 KB sends); and it lifts the constant for every other idea. Together with cells and the token-id closing hop it gives a measured 3–6× on a clustered swarm with a bit-identical stream, which is a publishable result on its own.

**The honest theorem for the paper:** single-stream exact latency is bounded below by (number of serial cuts) × (one-way hop), because the token's identity enters at device 1, every device's exact output requires the previous device's exact output, the identity path is Lipschitz-1 and injective at the ulp level, and no tolerance certificate exists without Lipschitz constants of the remaining depth. Iteration, Newton, Anderson, surrogates, asynchrony and replication cannot move the exact wavefront faster than one hop per hop. Only three levers exist: fewer cuts (placement, memory), cheaper cuts (transport), and throughput via pipelined drafts (already in place). Present the 2-model K ≈ 0.8–1.0·D measurement, SNLP's σ_max ≈ 44, and the 1-ulp propagation check as the evidence.

**Dead, with the reason:**
- The whole depth fixed-point family (#1, #2, #15, #16, #17, #32): K scales with D; tolerance stops commit wrong state; the star topology adds D-proportional fan-out and max-of-D RTT so it is often slower than the chain.
- The whole wavefront / space-time family (#3, #11, #18, #19, #30): the information front moves one device per round; provisional streams are stale-input outputs the user already rejected; certified throughput ≤ W/D per round.
- The asynchronous family (#4, #14, #28): async over a nilpotent chain equals the serial lap to 0.1%; residuals are small precisely while the state is wrong; #28's constructive half is PipeInfer and the repo's own continuous-speculation plan.
- The replication family (#6, #13, #23-stage, #26-fan-out/striping, #33): replicas are not bit-identical in this engine and first-arrival-wins has no verify step; even if fixed, the gain is a constant dominated by using the same memory to halve D.
- #5, #10, #12, #31, #25, #27: amortization, throughput-only, subsumed, or byte tricks that either break bit-identity or save no bytes.

**What I would not do:** build any of the star/host-sweep machinery before Experiment 0 on the 27B; the decision rule in idea 1's proposal (median K ≤ 6 at D=64) failed by ~9× on both models tested.

## 5. Refuted ideas, one line each

1. **Parareal-over-depth, star (#1):** measured K ≈ 0.8–1.0·D on two real models (β = 0.41–0.55 per block), star fan-out and max-of-64 RTT make it slower than the chain, and the residual+margin certificate is unsound.
2. **Depth-Newton tangent columns (#2):** probe directions must be chosen before the replies that define the Newton direction, so it is a lagged rank-r secant on a σ_max 18–44 chain; 3.2 MB host fan-out per iteration; D=4 slower than serial+MTP in every regime.
3. **Space-time wavefront relaxation (#3):** hash-certified exactness moves one device per round (K_conv = D), so certified throughput ≤ W/D per round = parity with serial+MTP at 28× GPU.
4. **Asynchronous chaotic relaxation (#4):** DES sim gives time-to-exact equal to the serial lap within 0.1%; small residuals mean the wave has not arrived; maxRetransmits=0 plus the change-gate loses the exact flag forever.
5. **Two-tier exactness certification (#5):** Tier A is 16-per-lap amortization on a kernel that is not bit-identical to the serial GEMV, Tier B needs a vacuous 63-layer Lipschitz constant, and the tiers cannot compose because Tier B leaves drifted state.
6. **Hedged multipath chain (#6):** chunked catch-up breaks recurrent bit-identity, heterogeneous replicas mix rounding lineages with no verify, and D=32 serial with the same memory beats hedged D=64.
7. **Cross-stream batching (#10):** throughput-only; per-stream latency is unchanged or 27–45% worse at Mac/phone column costs; kernel choice depends on N so streams are not reproducible.
8. **2-D speculative wavefront ROB (#11):** with rejected-branch guesses the round-to-round deltas are exactly zero for r < d, so the tolerance stop emits the rejected token's continuation; exact retirement is D rounds = serial at 64× GPU.
9. **Slipstream Q2 A-stream (#12):** hidden-state guesses cannot retire in < D rounds and Q2 errors are 2 orders above the token-preserving tolerance; the token half is ordinary quantized self-draft with realistic acceptance ≤ what MTP already gives.
10. **Disjoint Eager Execution replica chains (#13):** retired stream is assembled from non-bit-identical replicas; gain is A_tree(8)/A(1) ≈ 1.33 on tokens-per-lap; phones pledge 0.5 GB so C ≈ 33 and M ≈ 2.
11. **Tomasulo mesh (#14):** exact-bit columns recomputed at timing-dependent batch widths are not bit-identical; pre-round-D logits contain no token-t information; what remains is ring topology plus idea 2.
12. **Residual-parareal with Anderson (#15):** k = f·D with f set by per-block ρ (k = 8–27 at D=64, k = D at D=4); host f32 reassociation breaks the bit-exact front in 3078/5120 elements.
13. **Low-bit twin parareal (#16):** simulation gives k = 7–12 of 16 and the idea's own stopping rule diverges from serial (greedy 2/24, seeded 6/6); hub fan-out and a 160 ms twin sweep leave ~1 tok/s.
14. **Batched-JVP Newton-Krylov (#17):** a 1-ulp input change flips 4–89 output components, so zero residual only occurs at the exact front (k = D); the 16-free-columns premise is contradicted by GB10's own 3.3×.
15. **Windowed seq×depth fixed point (#18):** per-position stopping is unsound, chunkwise DeltaNet is not bit-identical to the serial recurrence, and lossy intermediate rounds slow the exact front; k = D.
16. **Margin-certified provisional emission (#19):** x_L^k carries zero bits of token t for k < D (verified numerically), so e_hat ≈ 0 certifies a stutter; strict mode is the D-round wavefront at 64× GPU.
17. **Hedged ring (#23):** stage replicas are exact only on identical hardware+driver+browser, diverse placement kills the min-of-2 gain, and at a deterministic 100 ms hop the gain is 0%; only path hedging survives.
18. **Cut-through column streaming with linearity head-start (#25):** group width selects a different kernel (coop/coop_b4/coop_b8/GEMM) with a different accumulation order, the f16 rms scalar adds 2^-11 error, and 6.4 of 8.56 s is still pure RTT.
19. **Coded stage replication (#26):** autotune-per-peer and vendor builtins make replicas non-identical, so MDS striping across them silently corrupts with zero detection at k-of-n; r-fold uplink cost exceeds the min-of-r gain.
20. **Precision-tiered speculative columns (#27):** exact, but the A+1 re-sent exact columns consume the entire byte budget, and 64 int4 re-quantizations of a residual stream with massive activations drive ρ far above 5%.
21. **Token-domain wavefront certificate (#28):** the constructive half is PipeInfer/SpecPipe and the repo's own continuous-speculation plan, batch width is timing-dependent, and the certificate omits device state lineage; the negative result on hidden-state Jacobi is correct.
22. **Space-time Jacobi two-tier certificate (#30):** the all-flags certificate fires at exactly round D+1 (1-ulp changes pass the identity path), every changed column must be shipped every round (640 KB/round/phone at D=64), Anderson breaks the lemma, and β_W is 3.3–8 not 1.2.
23. **Two-level Schwarz over clusters (#31):** the G-round exact bound equals the topology-sorted serial chain plus G·h_LAN at G× GPU, and argmax-stable early stops are wrong 29% of the time; the clustering itself is #24.
24. **Host-side learned surrogate parareal (#32):** Gander–Vandewalle needs ‖Δ‖ ≪ 0.016 at D=64 (unreachable for rank-64 affine), tolerance-gated resends create spurious certified fixed points, and bitwise certification costs D+1 star rounds = 2× the serial chain.
25. **Coverage-graph routing (#33):** per-token first-responder switching over non-bit-identical replicas yields a stream that matches no serial pass; overlap placement + shortest path + cached-input replay is Petals; hedging is a 1.2–1.3× constant over same-packing serial.
