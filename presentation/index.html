Why one device isn't enough

Nobody's phone holds 15 GB of GPU memory, and the cloud means an account, a key, and a retention policy. Prior work topped out around 8B in a tab.

SwarmLLMhow it works
Layer-split over WebRTC

The weights never move. A 10 KB baton does.

hidden state — 5,120 numbers, sent as f16 = 10 KB per hop
host keeps the tokenizer, embedding, LM head and sampler — text never leaves it
peers hold only their layer range, fetched straight from HuggingFace and cached
direct browser-to-browser over WebRTC; the signaling broker leaves after the handshake

SwarmLLMthe engine
From-scratch WebGPU engine

~50 hand-written WGSL kernels. Decode runs at the memory roofline.

Generating a token is memory-bound: you read every weight once. So the work is reading fewer bytes and reading them shoulder-to-shoulder — 64 threads sweep each row together, dequantize 4-bit blocks in registers, and reduce in shared memory.

The gap between the two lines is 0.5%. The remaining ~30 ms per token is dispatch and readback overhead — the kernels themselves are done on this GPU; speculation is what moves the number now.

SwarmLLMspeculation
Multi-token prediction

Guess ahead. Verify in the same pass. Lose nothing.

The model ships with a small draft layer. It proposes the next words; the full model verifies them all in one batched pass. A wrong guess only costs speed — output is bit-identical to plain decoding, and a correct guess saves a whole network lap.

draftThecapitalofFranceisLyon
verifyThecapitalofFranceisLyon
emitThe capital of France isParis
5 of 6 drafts accepted → 6 tokens for one lap. Measured: 85% acceptance on code at depth 3, 97% at depth 5. Depth adapts to what actually measures fastest on your link.
1 lap → up to 8 tokens · DeltaNet state snapshots make rejection exact6 / 10
SwarmLLMacross the internet
When devices aren't on the same Wi-Fi

Physics charges one ping per lap. So we make laps carry more.

Prefill sends 16 tokens per round. Decode chains up to 7 drafts so a slow link still moves ~6 tokens per lap. The room measures tokens per second at each depth and keeps whichever wins.
