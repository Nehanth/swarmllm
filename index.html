WHAT IS SWARMLLM

A group of devices,
running one AI together.

Big models like Qwen 3.8 are too large for any single phone or laptop. That is why AI lives in data centers.

SwarmLLM splits the model across every device in a room: each one downloads just its slice, and every word of the answer takes a lightning lap through all of them before it appears on every screen at once.

No install. No accounts. No servers. Your words never leave the room.
